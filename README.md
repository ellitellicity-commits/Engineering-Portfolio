### 💼 RetainIQ — The Revenue Guardian
> *B2B churn prediction platform · ThunderHacks 2026 Gold Sponsor Winner*

In SaaS, losing a single corporate client is a disaster. RetainIQ is an intelligent B2B retention engine that identifies high-risk subscriber accounts **in real-time** before churn becomes irreversible.

**Highlights**
- Real-time risk scoring across corporate accounts using predictive ML models
- One-click "save-the-deal" email trigger system built directly into the dashboard
- Engineered for B2B stakes — where one lost client can erase an entire quarter of revenue
- Built end-to-end with **Rishav Bawa** (Algoma University) and awarded the Gold Sponsor Challenge at ThunderHacks 2026

### 🌊 FloodNet Jamaica — The Island's Shield
> *NASA-integrated disaster early-warning system · Collaborative prototype*

Jamaica's topography gives residents near-zero evacuation time when flooding strikes. FloodNet Jamaica closes that gap by tapping NASA satellite data to forecast flood events before they happen.

**Highlights**
- Integrated **NASA Global Precipitation Measurement (GPM)** satellite data for real-time weather pattern simulation
- Built predictive flood-event models calibrated to Jamaica's unique topographic risk profile
- Designed to surface actionable early warnings for vulnerable communities with minimal time to respond
- Collaborative prototype developed alongside **Frank Zombre** and **Jacob Dinkha**
